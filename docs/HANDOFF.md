revision directories. This is code/workflow reproduction, **not** possession of the 2,624
real accepted records or the 44 candidate artifacts. Those remain in the private local
delivery and need a separate approved data-access channel.

The new unified packaging may be checked locally against existing stored outputs without placing
those inputs or per-record outputs in this repository. Such a parity receipt is packaging
evidence, not another dataset qualification or parser-research release.

The source inventory and staged content are checked before publication. No collaborator
permissions are changed automatically. Real-data sharing, repository visibility changes
and further distribution require separate decisions.
